*Draft — outline in progress.*

Every week a new framework is announced as the end of the previous one, and every week thousands of developers feel the same small panic: *am I falling behind?* This essay argues the panic is not information — it is the business model.

Planned sections:

- **The feed is a slot machine for professional anxiety** — variable rewards, engagement mechanics, and why "keeping up" is unwinnable by design.
- **Marcus and other people's opinions** — the *Meditations* as a manual for attention management written by the most-watched man in the world.
- **A Stoic filter for new technology** — three questions before learning anything: does it solve a problem I have? will it exist in five years? am I curious or just afraid?
- **Depth compounds, novelty doesn't** — the career math of learning one thing well versus surveying twenty.
